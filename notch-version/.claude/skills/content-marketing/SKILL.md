# Content Marketing & Copywriting

## Copywriting Principles for Agency Websites

### Value Proposition Formula
**[Who you help] + [What you do] + [Why it matters]**

Example: "We help SaaS companies build AI-powered digital experiences that drive growth and delight users."

### Headline Frameworks

**Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS)**
1. Problem: "Your website isn't converting visitors into customers."
2. Agitate: "Every day, potential clients leave without booking a call."
3. Solution: "We build conversion-optimized websites powered by AI that turn visitors into revenue."

**Before-After-Bridge (BAB)**
1. Before: Current pain state
2. After: Desired future state
3. Bridge: How your service gets them there

### CTA Copy Best Practices
- Use action verbs: "Start", "Get", "Book", "Launch", "Build"
- Include benefit: "Book a Free Strategy Call" not "Contact Us"
- Create urgency when genuine: "Limited spots available"
- Match CTA to page intent:
  - Home: "Book a Free Consultation"
  - Services: "Get a Custom Quote"
  - Case Studies: "Start Your Project"
  - Blog: "Subscribe for Insights"
  - Pricing: "Choose Your Plan"

## Page-Specific Copy Guidelines

### Homepage
- **Hero headline**: 6-10 words, clear value proposition
- **Social proof**: "120+ Projects Delivered | 98% Client Satisfaction"
- **Service overview**: 3-6 services, one sentence each
- **Trust signals**: Client logos, testimonials, stats
- **Final CTA**: Repeat primary action

### Services Page
- **Each service needs**: Title, 2-3 sentence description, 3-5 bullet points, relevant case study link, CTA
- **Use benefit-driven language**: "Increase conversions by 40%" not "We do A/B testing"
- **Include process overview**: How you deliver the service (3-5 steps)

### Case Studies
- **Structure**: Challenge > Solution > Results
- **Lead with metrics**: "43% increase in conversion rate"
- **Include client quote**: Social proof from the actual client
- **Visual evidence**: Before/after screenshots, dashboards

### Blog Posts
- **Title**: Include primary keyword, under 60 characters
- **Introduction**: Hook in first 2 sentences, state what reader will learn
- **Headers**: Use H2/H3 with keywords, make scannable
- **Length**: 1,500-2,500 words for SEO value
- **CTA**: Relevant to post topic, natural placement
- **Internal links**: Link to 2-3 relevant service or blog pages

### Pricing Page
- **Anchor pricing**: Show highest tier first or highlight middle tier
- **Feature framing**: Benefits, not features ("Priority support" not "Slack channel")
- **Remove friction**: FAQ section addressing common objections
- **Social proof near pricing**: "Trusted by 120+ companies"

## Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

### Form Optimization
- Minimum fields: Name, Email, Message (3 fields max for lead gen)
- Progressive disclosure: Ask for more info after initial engagement
- Real-time validation with helpful error messages
- Clear privacy statement near submit button
- Success state with next step information

### Social Proof Hierarchy
1. **Strongest**: Named testimonials with photos and company
2. **Strong**: Client logos of recognizable brands
3. **Good**: Star ratings and aggregate scores
4. **Supporting**: Project count, years of experience, team size

### Trust Signals
- Security badges (SSL, secure payments)
- Industry certifications and partnerships
- "No commitment" or "Free consultation" messaging
- Clear refund/satisfaction guarantee
- Team photos and real company address

## Email Sequence Templates

### Welcome Sequence (After Form Submission)
1. **Immediate**: Thank you + what to expect next
2. **Day 1**: Value-add content (relevant blog post or resource)
3. **Day 3**: Case study showcasing relevant results
4. **Day 5**: Direct CTA to book a call

### Nurture Sequence (Blog Subscribers)
- Weekly or bi-weekly industry insights
- Mix of original content and curated resources
- Occasional case study or success story
- Soft CTA to services every 3-4 emails

## Writing Style Guide

### Tone
- **Professional but approachable** — not corporate stiff, not casual sloppy
- **Confident without arrogance** — "We've delivered 120+ projects" not "We're the best agency ever"
- **Clear and direct** — short sentences, active voice, no jargon
- **Empathetic** — acknowledge client challenges before presenting solutions

### Formatting
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bullet points for lists of 3+ items
- Bold key phrases for scannability
- Use numbers for quantifiable claims
- One idea per paragraph
