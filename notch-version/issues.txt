3.2 — CCPA/CPRA Compliance (California)
Applies if ANY one of the following is true: annual gross revenue over $26.6M; process data of 100,000+ California residents annually; or derive 50%+ revenue from selling/sharing personal data.
Required CCPA Elements:
Categories of personal information collected in the past 12 months.
Sensitive personal information disclosure (separate section).
Sources of personal information.
Business/commercial purposes for collection.
Categories of third parties (distinguish: disclosures vs. sales vs. sharing).
“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link — must be conspicuous and homepage-accessible.
“Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information” link — required if collecting sensitive data.
Global Privacy Control (GPC) recognition — must honor browser opt-out signals.
Consumer rights section: right to know, delete, correct, opt-out, non-discrimination.
Penalties: $2,500/unintentional violation; $7,500/intentional violation or involving minors; up to $750/incident for data breaches (private right of action).
3.3 — COPPA Compliance (Children Under 13)
Applies if website is directed to children under 13 or you have actual knowledge of collecting data from children under 13. Requires verifiable parental consent BEFORE collection.
Penalties: Up to $53,088 per violation (FTC enforcement).
3.4 — CAN-SPAM Act (Email Marketing)
Required for ALL commercial emails sent by or on behalf of Soft Standards Inc.:
Accurate header information (From, To, routing must identify sender correctly).
Honest subject lines reflecting email content.
Clear identification as advertisement.
Valid physical postal address included in every email.
One-click unsubscribe mechanism that works for at least 30 days.
Honor opt-out requests within 10 business days.
Penalties: Up to $53,088 per email in violation.
3.5 — New York State Requirements
As a New York Corporation, additional state-level requirements include:
NY SHIELD Act: Requires “reasonable safeguards” to protect private information of NY residents. Must implement data security program with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.
NY General Business Law §899-aa: Data breach notification to affected NY residents “in the most expedient time possible” and to the NY Attorney General, DFS, and Division of State Police.
NY Department of Financial Services (23 NYCRR 500): If providing financial services, must comply with cybersecurity regulations.
3.6 — GDPR Compliance (International Users)
Applies if you offer services to EU/EEA residents, monitor their behavior (via cookies/analytics), or have employees in the EU. Key requirements:
Legal basis for processing (consent, contract, legitimate interest, etc.).
Data subject rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection.
International data transfer mechanisms (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, SCCs, or BCRs).
Right to lodge complaint with supervisory authority.
Data Protection Officer contact info (if applicable).
Penalties: Up to €20 million or 4% of global annual revenue (whichever is higher).
3.7 — Cookie Consent Requirements
Implement a cookie consent banner, especially if the site uses analytics (Google Analytics), marketing pixels, or any non-essential cookies:
EU visitors: Prior opt-in consent required before setting non-essential cookies. “Accept All” and “Reject All” buttons must be equally prominent. No pre-checked boxes.
California visitors: Opt-out model allowed (cookies can load, but provide opt-out). Honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals. Include “Do Not Sell or Share” link.
All visitors: Disclose cookie categories (strictly necessary, functionality, analytics, marketing, third-party).

4.2 — Implementation: Use Clickwrap, NOT Browsewrap
IMPORTANT: CLICKWRAP vs. BROWSEWRAP
Clickwrap (checkbox “I Agree”) has a ~70% court enforceability rate. Browsewrap (link in footer only) has only ~14% enforceability. Always use clickwrap for contact forms, checkout, and account creation.
Backend must log: timestamp, IP address, ToS version accepted, and user agent for each consent.
