Our Culture

Why Soft Standards Inc.

We've built a culture where great work happens naturally — and where people actually want to stay.

Remote First

Work from anywhere. We've been fully remote since day one, with team members across 12 countries and 8 time zones. Flexibility is built into everything we do — because life happens, and great work doesn't require an office.

Growth Focused

Annual learning budgets, conference tickets, and mentorship programs. We invest in your development because when you grow, we all win.

Team Driven

No rockstars, no egos. We collaborate openly, share credit generously, and win together. Everyone has a voice that matters.

Impact Oriented

Work on projects that move the needle. Every project we take on has a clear purpose and measurable outcomes — no busywork.
