Our Journey

The Story

2018

Founded

Started as a two-person studio with one mission: raise the bar for digital craft. Our first client was a local fintech startup that became our long-term partner.

2020

First Major Award

Recognized by Awwwards with a Site of the Day for our work on the Nexus Fintech rebrand — a turning point that put us on the global radar.

2022

50th Project Milestone

Crossed 50 completed projects and grew our team to 15. Expanded into mobile development and digital marketing disciplines.

Global Expansion

Opened remote hubs across three continents, serving clients in 12+ countries. 120+ projects delivered and counting.
